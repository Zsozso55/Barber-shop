Why your test came out terrible. You almost certainly uploaded the 2×2 reference sheet (4 faces in one image) into the try-on. The AI then tried to cut hair across a collage of four heads → a mess. Always upload ONE single, cropped, front-facing face — never the grid, never a 3/4 angle. Every prompt below makes a single clean portrait for exactly this reason.

What this pack gives you

Professional, single-image prompts for every place the app shows a face: (A) the hairstyle catalog heads — one consistent model wearing the same clothes, same hair colour, only the cut changes; (B) 3 barber profile pictures; (C) 3 client profile pictures; (D) a clean demo face for the try-on. Generate them in Nano Banana 2, then drop them into the app folders listed at the end — the app already looks for them.
